Project Echo – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026 · Developer: Gradolus IT · Contact: gradolusit@gmail.com

What the game collects

Nothing. Project Echo does not collect, store on any server, share or sell personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your progress (solved rooms, best moves, saved games, achievements, settings) is kept in a small file on your device only. Uninstalling the game deletes it. Android's own backup may include it if you have backups turned on; that is handled by Google under your account's settings.

Purchases

The full game is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play. The game only learns whether the purchase exists; it never sees your payment details. See Google's privacy policy for how Google Play processes payments.

Permissions

Vibration (short haptic feedback, can be turned off in Settings) and Google Play Billing (for the purchase). No access to location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Children

The game collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.

Project Echo – Politika zasebnosti

Zadnja sprememba: 31. oktober 2026 · Razvijalec: Gradolus IT · Stik: gradolusit@gmail.com

Kaj igra zbira

Ničesar. Project Echo ne zbira, ne hrani na strežnikih, ne deli in ne prodaja osebnih podatkov. Nima računov, oglasov, analitike ali sledenja.

Kaj ostane na napravi

Napredek (rešene sobe, najmanj potez, shranjene igre, dosežki, nastavitve) je shranjen le v majhni datoteki na tvoji napravi. Ob odstranitvi igre se izbriše. Če imaš vklopljeno varnostno kopiranje Androida, ga lahko vključi Googlova kopija pod nastavitvami tvojega računa.

Nakupi

Celotna igra je enkraten nakup, ki ga v celoti izvede Google Play. Igra izve le, ali nakup obstaja, podatkov o plačilu ne vidi nikoli. Kako Google Play obdeluje plačila, opisuje Googlova politika zasebnosti.

Dovoljenja

Vibracija (kratek odziv, izklopljiv v nastavitvah) in Google Play Billing (za nakup). Brez dostopa do lokacije, stikov, kamere, mikrofona ali datotek.

Otroci

Igra ne zbira podatkov od nikogar, tudi od otrok ne.

Spremembe

Če se politika spremeni, bo nova različica objavljena na tem naslovu z novim datumom.
