Project Echo – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026 · Developer: Gradolus IT · Contact: gradolusit@gmail.com

What the game collects

Nothing. Project Echo itself does not collect, store on any server, share or sell personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The Android version includes Google Play's own libraries for the purchase and the rating window; they are described below.

What stays on your device

Your progress (solved rooms, best moves, saved games, achievements, settings) is kept in a small file on your device only. Uninstalling the game deletes it. Android's own backup may include it if you have backups turned on; that is handled by Google under your account's settings.

If the game stops with an error, a short note (the game's version, the time and the error text) is kept on your device until the next start. It is never part of the backup.

Error reports

After an error the game may offer a SEND button. It opens your own e-mail app with the note ready to read and change. Nothing is sent unless you send that e-mail yourself. If you do, we receive your e-mail address and the text; we use them only to find the error and to answer you, and delete them on request.

Purchases

The full game is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play. The game only learns whether the purchase exists; it never sees your payment details. Google's billing library in the game connects to Google for this and may send Google technical data about the purchase service; we receive none of it. See Google's privacy policy for how Google processes this data.

Rating

Once, after a story scene, the game may ask Google Play to show its own rating window. Google Play decides whether it appears. What you enter there goes to Google Play as your review: public, or in a closed test visible only to the developer. The game never learns your answer. You can delete the review in your Google account.

Permissions

Vibration (short haptic feedback, can be turned off in Settings), Google Play Billing (for the purchase), and internet access and network state (used by Google Play's libraries; the game itself makes no connections). No access to location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Children

The game collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.

Project Echo – Politika zasebnosti

Zadnja sprememba: 25. november 2026 · Razvijalec: Gradolus IT · Stik: gradolusit@gmail.com

Kaj igra zbira

Ničesar. Project Echo sam ne zbira, ne hrani na strežnikih, ne deli in ne prodaja osebnih podatkov. Nima računov, oglasov, analitike ali sledenja. Različica za Android vsebuje Googlovi knjižnici za nakup in za okno z oceno; opisani sta spodaj.

Kaj ostane na napravi

Napredek (rešene sobe, najmanj potez, shranjene igre, dosežki, nastavitve) je shranjen le v majhni datoteki na tvoji napravi. Ob odstranitvi igre se izbriše. Če imaš vklopljeno varnostno kopiranje Androida, ga lahko vključi Googlova kopija pod nastavitvami tvojega računa.

Če se igra ustavi zaradi napake, na napravi do naslednjega zagona ostane kratek zapis (različica igre, čas in besedilo napake). V varnostno kopijo nikoli ne gre.

Poročila o napakah

Po napaki lahko igra ponudi gumb POŠLJI. Odpre tvojo e-pošto z zapisom, ki ga lahko prebereš in spremeniš. Dokler je ne pošlješ ti, se ne pošlje nič. Če jo pošlješ, dobimo tvoj e-poštni naslov in besedilo; uporabimo ju le za iskanje napake in odgovor, na zahtevo ju izbrišemo.

Nakupi

Celotna igra je enkraten nakup, ki ga v celoti izvede Google Play. Igra izve le, ali nakup obstaja, podatkov o plačilu ne vidi nikoli. Googlova knjižnica za nakupe se za to poveže z Googlom in mu lahko pošlje tehnične podatke o delovanju nakupov; mi jih ne dobimo. Kako Google obdeluje te podatke, opisuje Googlova politika zasebnosti.

Ocena

Enkrat, po prizoru zgodbe, lahko igra prosi Google Play, naj pokaže svoje okno za oceno. Ali se pokaže, odloči Google Play. Kar vpišeš, gre v Google Play kot tvoja ocena: javno, v zaprtem testu pa jo vidi le razvijalec. Igra tvojega odgovora ne izve. Oceno lahko izbrišeš v svojem Googlovem računu.

Dovoljenja

Vibracija (kratek odziv, izklopljiv v nastavitvah), Google Play Billing (za nakup) ter dostop do interneta in stanje omrežja (uporabljata ju Googlovi knjižnici; igra sama se nikamor ne povezuje). Brez dostopa do lokacije, stikov, kamere, mikrofona ali datotek.

Otroci

Igra ne zbira podatkov od nikogar, tudi od otrok ne.

Spremembe

Če se politika spremeni, bo nova različica objavljena na tem naslovu z novim datumom.
